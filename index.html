About

I am a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at Xidian University, advised by Weihao Yang and Qi Chen.

My research focuses on information theory, particularly entropy regions and their connections to polymatroids and combinatorial structures. I am also interested in automated methods for proving information inequalities.

Previously, I was a research assistant in the Department of Electronic Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and at Xidian University.

Research Interests

Entropy regions and information inequalities
Characterizing entropy functions on faces of the polymatroidal region and studying constraints beyond Shannon-type inequalities.
Combinatorial structures in information theory
Exploring connections between entropy functions and combinatorial constructions, including Latin squares and related structures.
Automated proving of information inequalities
Investigating language models and guided search for proving Shannon-type entropy inequalities.
